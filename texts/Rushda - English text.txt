=== PAGE 3: TITLE PAGE ===
RUSHDA AND THE SECRET POPCORN TRAIL
Written by Rushda At-Taqwaa Rahman

=== PAGES 4-5 ===
Title: The Secret

One rainy afternoon, Rushda was painting with Baba.

“Baba! I have a secret!” she whispered.

“What secret?” asked Baba.

“I can’t tell you. Then everyone will know!” Rushda giggled. “But I can SHOW you.”

She turned her painting around.

TRY IT: What do you think Rushda painted?

=== PAGES 6-7 ===
Title: A Very Wiggly Map

There was a BIG mountain. A LITTLE mountain. A golden treasure chest.

And between them was a long, wiggly trail of popcorn.

“It’s a treasure map!” said Rushda.

“How do we get there?” asked Baba.

Rushda ran her finger across the paper. “First left. Then right. Then S, S, S, S!”

TRY IT: Trace a big letter S in the air.

=== PAGES 8-9 ===
Title: POP! POP! POP!

POP!

One popcorn kernel jumped out of the painting.

POP! POP! Two more bounced across the floor.

“Baba,” whispered Rushda, “our map is moving!”

The room began to shimmer. The floor began to wiggle.

And WHOOSH! Rushda and Baba tumbled right into the painting.

TRY IT: Can you make three popcorn sounds?

=== PAGES 10-11 ===
Title: Where Did Our Powers Go?

They landed in a valley full of golden popcorn.

Rushda tried to make a little splash with her water power. Not a drop!

Baba waved his hands to make a breeze with his air power. Not a puff!

“Our powers are gone!” cried Baba.

Rushda pointed toward the tiny mountain. “We have to reach the treasure to get them back.”

Then she smiled. “Challenge accepted!”

TRY IT: none

=== PAGES 12-13 ===
Title: The Way Back

Baba looked down the winding trail.

“What if we find the treasure but can’t find the way home?”

Rushda stopped. She studied her map.

“I know! We leave little popcorn arrows behind us. Then we can follow them back!”

She laid three kernels on the ground, making a tiny arrow.

“Smart thinking,” said Baba. Rushda grinned. “Follow the map, Baba!”

TRY IT: Which way does an arrow point?

=== PAGES 14-15 ===
Title: The Hungry Dinosaur

ROOOAAAR!

A gigantic green dinosaur poked its head out from behind a rock.

Baba froze. Rushda listened.

GRRRRUMBLE! It was not an angry roar. It was a very hungry tummy.

“You need a snack!” said Rushda.

She offered the dinosaur some popcorn. CRUNCH! MUNCH! The dinosaur wagged its tail and moved aside.

TRY IT: Give your best friendly dinosaur roar.

=== PAGES 16-17 ===
Title: The Wall in the Way

Next came a wall. A HUGE wall. Almost as tall as the clouds!

“Over it?” asked Baba.

“Around it!” said Rushda. She checked the map again.

The trail curled left, then right, then left again.

“S, S, S, S!” they chanted, zigzagging through a hidden passage.

At last the wall was behind them. “We didn’t need to climb,” Baba said. “We needed to look!”

TRY IT: Follow the wiggly trail with your finger.

=== PAGES 18-19 ===
Title: The Hot, Hot Crack

RUMBLE-RUMBLE!

A glowing crack stretched across the path. Deep below was bubbling orange lava.

“Can we use your water power?” asked Baba.

Rushda shook her head. “Not until we find the treasure.”

She looked at the map, then at the hills.

“I know! We go the safe way around!”

And that is exactly what they did.

TRY IT: none

=== PAGES 20-21 ===
Title: A Popcorn Journey

Soon they reached a sparkling river. A little wooden boat waited by the bank.

“A sea journey!” said Baba as they climbed in.

“No, Baba!” Rushda laughed. “We’re not going on a sea journey.”

“We’re going on a POPCORN journey!”

Baba picked up an oar. “Should we row?”

Rushda leaned back and watched the popcorn-shaped clouds. “Nope. We just float.”

TRY IT: Can you pretend you are floating?

=== PAGES 22-23 ===
Title: Three Different Paths

At the end of the river, three paths waited.

One led to a dark cave. One climbed a prickly mountain. One entered a sparkly forest.

“Which one?” asked Baba.

Rushda almost closed her eyes to pick. Then she noticed something.

Three buttery kernels pointed toward the forest.

“The popcorn knows!” she said. “This way!”

TRY IT: Which path would YOU choose?

=== PAGES 24-25 ===
Title: The Golden Treasure

Beyond the sparkly forest stood the LITTLE mountain.

And on top of it sat a golden treasure chest.

“We found it!” cried Baba.

But the chest had a strange lock. It was shaped like a big letter S.

Rushda smiled. “I know this one.”

She traced the shape: “S, S, S, S!”

CLICK! The golden lid sprang open.

TRY IT: Trace the secret letter S!

=== PAGES 26-27 ===
Title: WHOOSH! SPLISH!

Inside danced two tiny lights: one silver, one blue.

The blue light touched Rushda. SPLISH! A ribbon of water swirled around her boots.

The silver light touched Baba. WHOOSH! A soft breeze lifted his hair.

“Your power is air,” Rushda said, “and my power is WATER!”

Together they made a shimmering rainbow.

“We did it!” laughed Baba.

TRY IT: none

=== PAGES 28-29 ===
Title: Following the Arrows

Then Baba looked at the faraway mountain.

“Now, how do we get home?”

Rushda unfolded her map. Along the paths, all the little popcorn arrows were waiting.

“Remember? I made a way back!”

They followed the arrows past the river, around the wall, and by the friendly dinosaur.

At the last turn, Rushda spotted a door that looked exactly like her painting.

TRY IT: Can you find the trail back to the beginning?

=== PAGES 30-31 ===
Title: One More Secret

POP!

Rushda and Baba landed on the living-room carpet.

There was the painting. The mountains. The treasure. The wiggly popcorn trail.

And beside Rushda’s crayons sat one real, buttery popcorn kernel.

“Was that adventure real?” whispered Baba.

Rushda winked. “It’s a secret.”

Then she picked up a purple crayon. “Baba? Ready for another challenge?”

Baba smiled. “Always.”

TRY IT: none
Closing line: THE END ... FOR NOW!
